# Research Sources

Internal research log for the factual content on this website. Each entry
records the source, what information was drawn from it, its reliability
classification, and when it was checked. This complements the shorter,
visitor-facing summary at `/research-sources` on the live site.

**Classification key:**
- **VERIFIED** — supported by a government source, an established
  encyclopedia, or corroborated news reporting.
- **TRADITIONAL/BELIEVED** — religious tradition or oral history, not
  independently verifiable as historical fact. Presented on-site with a
  "Tradition / Belief" badge, never as settled history.
- **REPORTED-BY-SINGLE-SOURCE / DISPUTED** — a claim found in only one
  source, or actively contested — generally omitted from the site, or
  described in explicitly hedged language when included at all.
- **NOT FOUND** — searched for but could not be confirmed; intentionally
  absent from the site rather than guessed. See `CONTENT_REQUIRED.md`.

Date checked for all entries below: **2026-08-19**.

This is the most sensitive research area on the site — see the caution
notes below and in `CONTENT_REQUIRED.md`.

| — | — | Birth date, guru-lineage, predecessor mahants, follower/disciple counts, awards, personal social media handles | **NOT FOUND** — absent from the site, not guessed |

**Editorial decision:** because his institutional title is described
inconsistently across sources, and because a live legal dispute exists
regarding succession within the broader Kabir Panth institutional tradition,
this site deliberately avoids asserting any single formal institutional
name or lineage claim for him. Language throughout uses the dignified,
neutral formulation "a respected religious guide associated with Kabir
Chaura, Maghar" rather than a specific unverified title.

## Images
